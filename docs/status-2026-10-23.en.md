# Prototype status — 23 October 2026

This note separates what has been tried on the hardware from what is only coded or simulated.
The publication remains a selected snapshot: access details, raw logs, private configurations and
the history of the source repository are not part of it.

## Rover

- The rover drives, with its chassis and electronics exposed; the shell is not fitted.
- The DT996 steering servo is out of service. The rover steers with the rear-wheel
  differential, servo at neutral (`direction.par_l_arriere` in `config/rover.toml`). An SG90 mount and
  its horn are designed ([CAD](../cao/prototype_final/README.md), in French), without validation under load.
- **Indoor autonomous navigation, 21 October, with the GX10**: exploration started by the
  instruction "explore la pièce" ("explore the room"), then an autonomous round trip on the lidar map.
- How exploration works: the Pi computes several next points in the known space,
  the GX10 picks one based on the map and the observations, the Pi re-validates that choice, drives
  and keeps control of safety. The model does not drive the motors directly.
- A scene analysis on the GX10 took about 6 to 9 s during these tests; the target period of
  3 s is not reached.
- Spatial memory: an object must be seen again in two separate visual analyses to be
  confirmed; the fast detector alone does not confirm a landmark.

Not validated: outdoor mission on grass ([planned protocol](../software/rover/TEST_TERRAIN_2026-10-22.md), in French),
false-positive rate of the perception, calibration of the axes and bias of the inertial measurement unit.

## Drone-carrier trailer

The trailer is built from the [published printable parts](../cao/prototype_final/remorque/README.md) (in French).
Its two flaps are driven by servos from the ESP32-C5 (ROVER_4 firmware, outputs D2/D3);
the firmware is flashed on the rover's ESP. The software estimates the hitch angle by odometry,
without a sensor, and includes the trailer's footprint in the safety supervisor.

Not validated: towing in an autonomous mission, reversing with the trailer hitched, actual flap position (the
command is timed, with no position feedback).

## Drone

The drone's BLE video stream is received on the Pi and image recognition works without flying.
The drone's battery level is not yet measured reliably: the field
first read as the battery is a model identifier. The command frames were identified
by offline analysis of the original app; real piloting stays disabled until
neutral, axes, rate and link loss are validated on the bench.

**Autonomous flight is not validated.** The take-off, photo lap and landing mission exists only in
[simulation](../software/rover/DRONE_MISSION.md) (in French).

## GX10

Eight models are served on the GX10's GB10:

| Role | Model |
| --- | --- |
| Agent, vision | Qwen3.6-35B-A3B NVFP4 |
| Embodied pointing | RoboBrain 2.5 (4B) |
| Detection | D-FINE |
| Monocular depth | Depth Anything V2 (metric) |
| Segmentation | SAM 2.1 |
| Transcription | Qwen3-ASR (1.7B) |
| Speech synthesis | Qwen3-TTS (0.6B) |
| Image embeddings | Qwen3-VL-Embedding (2B) |

Status by role: [MODELES.md](../software/rover/MODELES.md) (in French); configuration:
[deploiement/gx10](../software/rover/deploiement/gx10/README.en.md). During the ASUS session, the GX10
was remote and reached over a private network: a complete off-network deployment in the field
has not been demonstrated.

## Public simulation

<https://hugoprigent.com/fieldmind/> serves the rover's web station with simulated hardware. The
[`public_demo.py`](../software/rover/fieldmind/public_demo.py) server creates one simulated rover per
browser and initializes no hardware driver. It uses the GX10 if it is reachable, otherwise
another provider if one is configured, otherwise it runs without AI. Its deployment and keys
are not in this repository. Project page: <https://hugoprigent.com/projets/fieldmind>.

## Limits

A feature present in the code, a successful simulation or a successful AI call do not amount to
field validation. The repository's tests check the software contracts and the firmware
build; they cover neither driving the real rover, nor towing, nor flying the drone.
Commands and results: [validation.en.md](validation.en.md).
