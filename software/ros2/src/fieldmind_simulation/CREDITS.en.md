# 3D Model Credits

Worlds generated by `generer_monde` reference models from [Gazebo Fuel](https://fuel.gazebosim.org/1.0/OpenRobotics/models) published by Open Robotics. They are downloaded by Gazebo on first launch (cached `~/.gz/fuel`); none are copied into this repository.

| Model | Author | License |
|---|---|---|
| Pine Tree, Oak tree | Open Robotics | CC0 1.0 |
| House 1, House 2, House 3, Collapsed House | Open Robotics | CC0 1.0 |
| Hatchback, Pickup, Standing person, Construction Barrel | Open Robotics | CC0 1.0 |
| Falling Rock 1, 2, 3, 5 | Open Robotics | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

The "Falling Rock" rocks are used under the CC BY 4.0 license (attribution: Open Robotics, resized models). The terrain, ground texture, water, fallen logs, stumps, bushes, and fences are generated by `generateur_monde.py`.
